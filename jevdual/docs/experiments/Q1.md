# Q1. What signal should trigger deliberation?

Status: pre-registered 2026-09-21. Depends on Q6 traces and step labels.

## Observation

On the fixture site Jev's choice confidence saturated while the situation nouls did the work:
`stuck` flagged the form loops and `destructive` stopped the delete. The literature frames
arbitration as a meta-reasoning policy conditioned on the fast policy's uncertainty [A1] with a
separate metacognitive module choosing the solver [A4][A8], and offers a measurement:
metacognitive sensitivity, whether a confidence signal separates correct from incorrect actions
(meta-d′, ROC) [A5].

## Hypothesis

Calibrated situation nouls predict a wrong S1 step with AUROC at least 0.10 higher than choice
confidence, because confidence saturates on easy menus.

## Predictions

If true: AUROC(best noul or the effects diff) − AUROC(op confidence) ≥ 0.10 on the labelled steps,
with the bootstrap interval excluding 0.

If false: op confidence matches or beats every noul, or no signal reaches AUROC 0.7.

## Design

Offline over the Q6 traces. Signals per S1 step: op confidence, target confidence, each noul
(`goal_done`, `stuck`, `needs_reasoning`, `destructive`), the effects diff. Labels: roughly 300
steps hand-labelled right or wrong, stratified to include S1-only's confident wrong dones.
Compute ROC, AUROC, meta-d′ per signal; paired bootstrap on AUROC differences. `evals/sweep.py`
already replays traces; the metrics module adds the curves.

Primary metric: AUROC difference. Guardrail: labels are made before any curve is looked at.

## Decision rule

- Supported: arbiter thresholds are set from the best signal's ROC at a pre-chosen operating point
  (false-escalation rate ≤ 20%), replacing the hand-set numbers in `arbiter.toml`.
- Falsified with confidence winning: keep the confidence floors and drop nouls that add nothing.
- No signal reaches 0.7: the arbiter needs a new signal (a verification-style noul on the proposed
  action's precondition), not new numbers.

### Preliminary look, 2026-09-21, heuristic labels only (not the pre-registered test)

`evals.metrics --labels` on `results/live-dev-v0-20260921-110328/labels.csv`: 102 S1-acted steps
with heuristic labels (17 wrong), no human labels yet. AUROC for predicting a wrong step: op
confidence 0.53, target confidence 0.61, `needs_reasoning` 0.61, `stuck` 0.78, `goal_done` 0.28
(high `goal_done` goes with right steps), `destructive` 0.50. Two caveats that make this a look,
not evidence: the heuristic marks a step wrong partly from the *next* step's `stuck` verdict, which
inflates `stuck`'s score; and acted steps are range-restricted on confidence (they already passed
the floors), which deflates the confidence scores. The escalated steps (224) have no label at all
without a person. Human labels on the sheet are the next input.
