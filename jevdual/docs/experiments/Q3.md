# Q3. Is a small calibrated verifier better than an LLM judge?

Status: pre-registered 2026-09-21. Depends on Q6 traces and trajectory labels. Update 2026-09-21: the judge
arm is upstream browser-use's judge prompt run by the System 2 model; it already runs on every dual
and stock eval row and its verdict is recorded (`docs/upstream-reuse.md`), so the baseline costs nothing extra.

## Observation

Adding one question, "does the task ask for an answer?", removed every false completion on both
splits. But the current verifier also over-rejects: 13 of 23 dev passes ended UNVERIFIED because
multi-step requirements are judged on the final page. LLM judges over-validate agent behaviour
(agreement bias) [V5]; the best computer-use verifier reached near-zero false positives with
non-overlapping rubric criteria and separated process from outcome [V1]; an explicit
per-constraint ledger cut under-verified answers by up to 26% [V17]; terminal commitment deserves
its own score [V15].

## Hypothesis

A Jev constraint ledger with claim checks has a false-accept rate at most half a Muse judge's, at
under a tenth of the judge's cost, and the two combined beat either alone.

## Predictions

If true: FA(ledger) ≤ 0.5 × FA(judge), cost(ledger) < 0.1 × cost(judge), FA(both) ≤ min(FA).

If false: FA(judge) ≤ FA(ledger), or the ledger's false-reject rate exceeds the judge's by more
than 5 points (guardrail breach).

## Design

Offline over roughly 200 Q6 trajectories hand-labelled complete or not, including S1-only's
confident wrong dones and stock's deletes. Arms: (a) Jev ledger over the trajectory (the ledger
mechanism from Siftable task 7cf5ee7b, so this waits on that work) plus evidence-atom claim checks;
(b) Muse judge with a published judge prompt as the fair baseline; (c) both, accept only if both
accept. Metrics: false-accept, false-reject, cost per verdict, all with bootstrap intervals.

Primary metric: false-accept rate. Guardrail: false-reject within 5 points of the judge.

## Decision rule

- Supported: the ledger stays the default; the judge is used only where the ledger says verify.
- Falsified on FA: the judge becomes the verifier and the ledger a pre-filter, if cheaper overall.
- Guardrail breach: the ledger is re-designed (per-requirement evidence over the trajectory) before
  a rerun; no threshold tuning on the labelled set.
