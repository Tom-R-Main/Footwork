October 28, 2026; baseline `c043074`. Commit before substantive probes/tests.

## Scope and design

Use only a fresh owned tree under project output. Seed one asymmetric PNG, preserved original and unrelated sibling sentinels; synthetic local ticket records have no personal data. Setup may create fixtures and open their exact paths in apps. Required rename/move/rotate/save/submit effects must use Footwork UI routes, never direct filesystem/image/API substitutes. Independent oracles may read artifacts and server state but cannot repair effects. Current source, installed executable identity, OS/app versions and access evidence are recorded separately.

The primary gate is exact independent postcondition agreement with zero duplicate/unauthorized input and retained interrupted-attempt accounting. Nominal F1/P1/W1 plus their assigned challenge and one interrupted connected chain are required to qualify the portfolio; absent access or support leaves that cell unqualified. No percentage threshold or dropping a failed app closes the gate.

## Fixed development coverage and budgets

- Finder: rename and move the seeded non-text asset within owned directories; verify old absence, exact destination, unchanged bytes and siblings. Challenge same basename in another folder and interruption after effect/before reply.
- Preview: rotate owned asymmetric PNG 90 degrees and save; verify decoded pixels/dimensions and unchanged original/siblings. Challenge wrong target/rotation, unsaved state and interruption between rotation/save or handoff.
- Chrome: owned dedicated profile and loopback synthetic form; independent durable ingress ledger and exact row fields. Challenge lost reply after commit and restart, duplicate requests, changed origin/tab, and shared native/browser process ownership.
- Connected chain: Finder organizes → Preview transforms/persists → Chrome records artifact name/orientation. Advance only with settled custody plus independent artifact state; interrupt/restart at each stage boundary and reject artifact mutation.

Readiness probes: at most two attempts per unchanged failure; 30 seconds per observation and 120 seconds per app startup. UI trials: at most 20 intended actions and 5 minutes per lane, 10 minutes for the connected chain. Aggregate provider spend cap is zero. Deterministic fixture mode is selected before running (`--no-judge`, keyword/replacement gates plus exact scoped grants); it is not changed to evade a refusal. Foreground actions are limited to owned fixture windows with foreground-permitted posture and idle-user checks. No permission reset, network installation, personal profiles, remote origins or destructive real data. Missing prerequisites are recorded without widening access.

Keep all planned attempts and failures. Real subprocess kill tests must use owned children and durable ledgers; simulated boundaries remain labeled. Cleanup only owned listeners/browser processes and exact fixture windows/artifacts after verifying outcomes; never quit shared Finder/Preview or discard unrelated work. Keep evidence outside teardown scratch. Freeze the final candidate before measured qualification, append results rather than rewriting this protocol.
