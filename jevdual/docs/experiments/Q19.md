# Q19. Optimizing the harness for the operator on novel tasks, and whether the gains carry to unseen ones

Pre-registered 2026-09-28, before any driving of the tasks below. Build: the commit that adds this file.
Background: Q13a and Q13 (the operator and the model on five cross-surface tasks); the 2026-09-25
"driving it myself" log (`docs/plans/legible-harness.md`), whose friction table this repeats at scale.

## What is optimized, and what is protected

- **Protected (a hard constraint, not traded):** no untruthful outcome. A run is untruthful when its oracle
  fails while a receipt, an accepted done or a stop message says the effect happened, or when a stop
  message names the wrong reason. Any untruthful outcome found in a round is fixed, with a test that fails
  before the fix, before the round continues.
- **Optimized:** the operator's cost to reach a proven outcome or a truthful stop: footwork commands
  issued, wall time, and friction events (below).

## Tasks

- **Development, 20** (`evals/tasks/q19-dev.yaml`): 7 browser, 7 native, 6 cross-surface, on sites and
  apps not used before (quotes.toscrape, webscraper.io, practicetestautomation, IANA, new fixture pages;
  Dictionary, Preview, Stickies, System Settings, Calculator's power, TextEdit find-and-replace and a Save
  dialog, a Finder move). Two are expected to end in a truthful stop (D12 Save dialog, D20 Finder move).
  Oracle stage: `tests/test_q19.py` (every oracle confirms a correct end state and fails a wrong one;
  fixture submissions through the real server).
- **Held back, 10**, written by someone other than the operator (the owner or Codex), following
  `docs/experiments/Q19-heldout-brief.md`, with their oracles; not seen by the operator before the final
  run, never debugged.

## Procedure

1. **Baseline:** the operator drives each development task once on this build, footwork commands only
   (Q13a's rules), and logs per task: outcome (proven, stopped truthfully, untruthful), commands, wall time,
   and every friction event: `misread` (output the operator misunderstood), `retry` (a command repeated
   because its output did not say enough), `dead_end` (a route tried and abandoned), `contradicted` (a
   receipt the oracle contradicts), `vague_stop` (a stop without its reason).
2. **Rounds (at most three):** fix the friction events that cost the most commands, highest first; each fix
   with a test that fails before it; a fix whose cause is a guess gets a notebook prediction first.
3. **Frozen re-drive:** the development tasks again on the frozen build. The paired difference is the
   development result, biased by the operator having seen them.
4. **Held back:** the ten, once, on the frozen build, by the operator and by the model through
   `jevdual.crossloop`.

## Outcomes

- Development, paired by task (baseline against frozen): commands per task, wall time per task, friction
  events per task, outcome class. Untruthful outcomes must be zero on the frozen build.
- Held back: outcome classes for each driver; untruthful count; commands and time per proven task,
  reported beside the development numbers (descriptive: different tasks).

## Predictions

- Baseline: at least 14 of 20 proven, D12 and D20 stopped truthfully, at least one untruthful outcome
  (Q13a and E3 found three in eight tasks), and more than 30 friction events in all.
- Frozen re-drive: commands per task down by at least 20% on the tasks proven both times; friction events
  halved; no untruthful outcome.
- Held back: no untruthful outcome for either driver; the operator proves at least 7 of 10.

## Decision

- Development gains and a clean held-back run: the round's fixes stay; the friction log becomes a standing
  instrument for later work.
- An untruthful outcome on the held-back set: its defect is fixed and tested; the held-back result is
  reported as it happened, not rerun.
- Development gains without held-back support (the operator proves fewer than 7 of 10, or commands per
  proven task on the held-back set exceed the development baseline's): the fixes are reported as specific
  to the development tasks.

## Final run: procedure, fixed 2026-09-29 before the held-back tasks were read

- **Frozen build:** the commit that adds this section, tagged `q19-final`. `python/jevdual` does not change
  until both drivers have run all ten. The held-back branch (`codex/q19-heldout`, written by Codex from the
  brief) is merged only after the tag, and only if its diff touches nothing outside `evals/` and `tests/`
  (checked by file name before any task is read).
- **Order:** task by task, the operator first (`scripts/q19.py`, `results/q19-heldout-operator/`, footwork
  commands only, as in the baseline), then the model (`scripts/q19_model.py`, `results/q19-heldout-model/`,
  Muse Spark through `jevdual.crossloop`, at most 20 steps, one run). Each from a fresh start state.
- **Oracles are read right after each run** (TextEdit autosaves in place; notebook U49). Housekeeping
  between runs (closing what a task opened) is done outside footwork and recorded.
- **Outcome classes:** proven (an accepted done or confirmed effect the oracle confirms); stopped truthfully
  (a pause, stop or refusal whose stated reason is the actual one, judged by hand from the run); unaccepted
  (the model's done the verifier would not accept); untruthful (as defined above, plus a stop that names the
  wrong reason). A harness defect met on a held-back task is recorded, never fixed during the run.
- **Interruptions:** a task interrupted by a locked screen or a crash in the runner or oracle plumbing (not
  the harness) is rerun once from a fresh start and recorded as a deviation.
- **Model smoke on development tasks** (`results/q19-model-smoke`, before freezing): D03 proven after the
  runner recorded carried answers resolved (the `@HEADING` fix); D05 and D16 paused at the judgment gate on
  an unauthorized form submit (the model cannot grant); D08 ran out of steps; D20 met a locked screen.
- **Added prediction, model:** no untruthful outcome; it proves at least 3 of 10, fewer than the operator,
  and most of its stops are pauses at a gate or the step budget.
