## S8 · summary · 2026-09-27 16:11
Established: `footwork grant <the words do takes> [--uses N]` issues a grant against the current observation for one operation, on one resource (the page URL or the window's document, and the element's role and label), with these arguments (SHA-256), while the element holds its current value, for N dispatches (default 1). `do` consumes a grant only when every part matches at dispatch, and a pause that no grant clears prints, per open grant, the part that differed. Tests show it does not transfer to another document or page (a navigated tab included), another element with the same label, changed arguments, a changed value, or a retry beyond its uses; through the real commands on the simulated Mac, one granted Delete is pressed once and the retry pauses with "no uses left", and a relabelled second element does not inherit the grant. Grants never bind a secret's value. `start` clears them.
Uncertain: the native resource is the window's AXDocument or else its title, untested live; label grants (`--authorize`) remain as the broad form, since the experiments' task files depend on them.
