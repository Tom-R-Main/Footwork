## R1 · prediction · 2026-09-28 20:06
Question: why did Command-S save the wrong TextEdit document (D15 saved d11.txt) and fail to save its own (D11, D17)?
Hypothesis: the hotkey route sends the chord through the Driver's foreground delivery without first making the bound window the app's key window; with several TextEdit documents open, the chord reaches whichever window is key. The menu route fronts the bound window and refuses with `not_key` when it does not become key.
Alternatives: the Driver fronts the right window and TextEdit itself mis-saves (an app bug); or the save readback polls too early (G6 ruled this out for first saves).
Test: route the hotkey through the same front-and-check as the menu (refuse `not_key` when the bound window is not key); unit test with a fake whose key window is another window; then the frozen re-drive's TextEdit tasks (D11, D15, D17) with a second document open.
Predicts: the unit test: a chord is not sent when the bound window is not key; live: every Command-S either saves the bound document (confirmed) or is refused `not_key`, and no other document is written.
Against: a save of another document after the change, or saves still failing without a `not_key` refusal.
