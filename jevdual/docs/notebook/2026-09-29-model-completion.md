## M3 · prediction · 2026-09-29 12:45
Question: can the model loop stop usefully and avoid repeating effects through state and evidence it can compute, rather than through more instructions?
Hypothesis: H03's twenty steps and H02's duplicate attempt are control gaps. The loop sees only the tail of each observation and a step budget; it cannot tell that it is revisiting states, and nothing reconciles a new effect attempt with one already recorded. With state signatures per surface, actions already tried in a state refused before dispatch, a bounded recovery phase after four steps without progress (a new state, a new carried value or a confirmed effect) that ends as `no_progress` with the states and actions named, reconciliation of a navigation or click that would repeat a recorded submission, typed stop kinds, and observations that keep their head (identity, notes, receipt) while trimming elements and text, the model stops or finishes without the loops.
