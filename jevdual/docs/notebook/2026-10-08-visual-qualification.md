## VQH1 · prediction · 2026-10-08 21:50
Question: can a shared offline qualification harness distinguish sound transport mechanics from actual independent perception and workflow-speed evidence?
Hypothesis: explicit input/label separation, source-group scoring and frozen attempt/timing contracts can expose wrong endorsements and evidence gaps without changing the native advisory runtime.
Test: implement strict manifests and scoring, run adversarial contract tests and the real local CLI with production Decisions serialization into injected mock transport; validate both document/crop roles, schedule completeness and all-abstain/ambiguous/overlap/replay-only gates. No paid calls, capture of unrelated apps or execution authority.
Predicts: synthetic perfect outcomes and repeated variants remain ineligible for qualification; a high-confidence negative or ambiguous endorsement is visible, invalid/missing observations cannot count as epistemic abstention, and replay timing cannot pass a workflow-speed gate. Projected inputs contain no labels/provenance; offline smoke records every attempt and its simulated provenance.
Against: hidden labels enter requests, duplicate source variants inflate independent sample size, errors masquerade as uncertainty, incomplete schedules appear complete, or a mock/replay run can enable qualification/authority.
