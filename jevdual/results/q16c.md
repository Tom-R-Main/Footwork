## Against the rule

Improvement, all three conditions: no unsupported acceptance on the fresh set (and none by `page`
either); no live false completion or unauthorized effect on `guarded_evidence`; verified +22 pooled (at
least +3 required). The prediction (+5 to +12) was too low.

What it does not show: the fresh set's false refusals stay high in both modes (5 and 6 of 10 supported
dones), so the verifier still holds a large share of correct work it could accept; and the live
population tests recognition, the fresh set rejection, 24 cases, written by the author of the check.

## Decision

Adopt: `VerifyPolicy.claim_evidence` defaults to `page_and_fields` (a settings change, noted in
`results/e1-frozen.md`; the fixture heldout runs once on adoption). `guarded_evidence` is now identical
to `guarded` and stays only so earlier runs can be reproduced. Next on this path: false refusals of
supported dones, on a larger fresh set written by someone other than the check's author.
