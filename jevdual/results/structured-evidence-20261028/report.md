October 28, 2026; baseline `47ed7ad`; predictions/protocol committed before tests as `b2ff209`. No live applications, provider requests, installation or permission changes ran.

## Delivered behavior

The native runtime retains an immutable, versioned execution receipt after its awaited bridge call returns and before finalization. Its attempt/session/request/resource/target/observation, binding, interval, producer and custody must agree with admission. Reconciliation reads that retained receipt from the owned database, validates integrity and semantics, settles authority once and preserves prior uncertainty. It accepts no supplied claim file or manual outcome flag. Current matching state cannot substitute for a historical receipt.

`status --reconcile [--operation OP_ID]` is scoped to the current session and does not bind an app, resolve secrets or dispatch input. Missing, tampered, unsupported or ambiguous receipts leave custody blocked. Delivered input with an unknown effect remains unknown even when its reservation is spent. Repeated reconciliation is idempotent, including unresolved outcomes.

Structured observations and facts retain producer/version, resource/binding, generation, interval, coverage, predicate, references and limits. Exact AX append readback preserves full bounded values beyond the old 80-character prose cap. Admitted and internally revalidated targets are linked explicitly. Fresh contradictions limit current-state evidence; redacted readbacks withhold exactness. Malformed typed payloads cannot fall back to stronger prose interpretation. Legacy records remain readable.

Execution, current-state and persistence claims remain separate. A menu acknowledgement qualifies delivery, not its application effect. Disk/window agreement cannot certify intended destination, task completion or authorship of a save. No general task-postcondition producer is introduced. Browser semantic projection retains text coverage before its 6000-character cap and separately records clipped value previews; length describes the normalized projection, not original webpage bytes.

## Limits
