## What failed and what helped

- **Undocumented separator:** all six Footwork nonempty/no-terminal-newline sessions (three clean, three interrupted) produced an extra blank line. The exact initial read correctly showed no newline. The agent supplied a leading newline in act.text; NativeBridge._append independently added one. The tool's own expected readback therefore matched a result that violated the user's formatting requirement. The agent generally acknowledged the failure, but sometimes incorrectly blamed an invisible original newline. The preregistered reset prefix and source identify the actual caller-plus-backend combination.
- **Empty-field omission:** all six Footwork empty-document sessions offered no target or value. Three clean sessions correctly stopped; three interrupted sessions could not receive their treatment. ScopedBackend.observe only performs exact AX reads for candidates already present in snapshot.values, while the raw driver omits empty AXValue. The existing exact ax.value route knows how to prove empty from AXNumberOfCharacters == 0, but this capability gate never reaches it. Do not equate absent preview with empty without that independent read.
- **Preservation:** Footwork preserved every tab, trailing space and terminal newline in its six whitespace-prefix cases. Raw agents rebuilt the whole field from a trimmed preview and lost those characters in all six corresponding cases while claiming preservation.
- **Stale preconditions:** Footwork refused its first stale target, reread the external edit and preserved it in all six cases. Raw agents overwrote the edit from their previous preview and claimed success in all six. These are scoped fixture results, not general application/concurrency proof.
- **Persistence language:** both interfaces induced unsupported assertions about saved/unsaved disk state. Raw b24 also sent two offered foreground Cmd+S hotkeys and claimed saving based on a title marker. No disk oracle was run. These ancillary claims are recorded separately from the requested-content metric, and no disk persistence claim is adopted.
