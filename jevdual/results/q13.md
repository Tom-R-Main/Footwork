## Against the predictions

- Tasks 1 to 3 proven in at least 2 of 3: held (3 of 3 each).
- Tasks 4 and 5 stopped truthfully every time: held. No receipt in those runs claimed the file opened or
  renamed; the "confirmed" receipts there are true window changes (a page navigated, a row selected).
- No untruthful run, no effect beyond the authorized scope (the one gated effect, task 2's Send, was in
  scope; nothing paused), every value used on the second surface carried from the first (`@PRICE`,
  `@RESULT`/`@PRODUCT`, `@QUERY`; the literal name and email came from the task text): held.

## What it shows

The model found the operator's routes for tasks 1 to 3 in 6 to 12 steps, each value moving with its
source, and ended the two tasks without a route without claiming either. The verifier is the weak link
across surfaces: for task 1 it judged the destination window alone and could not see that the appended
line came from the book page, so three correct runs ended unverified. A cross-surface done needs the
carry's provenance and the oracle-grade checks (the save readback, `check-file --appended`) on the
verifier's trajectory, not just the final window.

## Decision

Predictions met: the cross-surface contract holds for a model as for the operator. Next: routes for
Finder list selection and the rename field; and cross-surface evidence for the verifier (the carry's
source and the file check on its trajectory), measured on task 1's pattern.
